The battery is the single most lied-about spec in this market. Two rules keep the table below honest:

- **Claimed and observed are separate facts, never merged.** A vendor "72 h" is not a datapoint about reality; a parent's "died at hour 11" is.
- **A battery figure without a measurement window and tracking state is meaningless.** "Two days" with tracking off and the watch shut down overnight is not "two days" — a real school day is a 9.5 h window with tracking on. Where owners report a window, the research documents keep it.
