## Verdict

No country gate: signup is email-only, so nothing exists to exclude Romania. But B20, its selling point, is vendor-claimed and disputed, and 2024–2026 owners report support that never answers. Romania: likely, tier 2.

## Does it work in Romania?

iOS app: live in the Romanian App Store with Romanian localisation. Signup: email, captcha, password. "Set your account region to North America, regardless of your physical location." Android app removed from Play (2026-01-31 to 2026-08-19, cause unverified); Android parents must sideload. No Romanian user report; APN set by hand.

## Connectivity

Three contradictory vendor band lists: the product page includes B20 and B3; the tech-spec KB lists only B1, B3, B5, B7, B8, no B20. No FCC ID or CE/RED declaration exists: B20 remains unverified. B28 buys nothing in Romania (5G n28). VoLTE present device-side; Romanian provisioning UNKNOWN. GSM 850/900/1800/1900 gives a 2G path; CSFB fallback inferred, unverified. Owner report: barely held a signal indoors (Play, 2023-01-06).

## Positioning

GPS, Wi-Fi and LBS; no GLONASS, Galileo or BeiDou. GPS 5–10 m, Wi-Fi 50–100 m, LBS 50–1000 m (vendor). No live tracking; manual, 10 min or 1 hour. Indoors "often showed my location darting across the street" (SafeWise, 2025-01-08); outdoors fine.

## Battery

Claimed: 2–3 days in one vendor KB, 1–3 days in another (800 mAh). Observed: 96+ h in a week of wear (SafeWise, 2025-01-08) at 10/60-minute updates; one source, better than claimed.

## Buying

Vendor-direct only: $149.99 / £149.99 (2026-08-19); no EU retailer. RO-geo page: 689 lei, buy buttons OUT OF STOCK, same day. Shipping 15-40 days vs 14-day returns; owners report returns unanswered.

## Watch out for

- Sound Guardian covert listen cannot be disabled; only mitigation: withhold Family Member status.
- App stem com.tgelec matches brands in DEF CON 34's shared-backend research; Angel Watch itself unnamed (unverified inference).
- Data hosting UNKNOWN; the privacy policy ignores the watch.
- WhatsApp reaches beyond the family group; removability unknown.
