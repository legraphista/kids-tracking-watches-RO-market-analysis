## Verdict

Strong radio: Romania-complete bands, including B20. Risks: a 67 g case reviewers call bulky on a child's wrist, no Romanian carrier or data-hosting region named, and the sole Romanian owner rating it 1 star. Plan on nightly charging.

## Does it work in Romania?

Status: likely. imoo's EU store ships to Romania, yet its supported-countries page omits it and names no Romanian operator. The only Romanian report (eMAG, 13 Aug 2026): *"Când suni normal este ok"* ("normal calls are fine"). Unverified: +40 registration (a 2025 report: the SMS code never arrived) and VoLTE provisioning.

## Connectivity

Romania's needed bands (B20, B3, B1, B8) are all present; B20, Orange's only sub-1 GHz LTE layer, decides indoor and rural coverage. VoLTE: "Supported" per imoo's spec table, unverified on Romanian operators. Voice falls back to 2G CSFB while Orange and Digi still run 2G. The Romanian owner reports video calls dropping on weak signal.

## Positioning

GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo, QZSS, Wi-Fi and a barometer for floors. Update interval: UNKNOWN. Three German reviews found indoor positioning reliable; the Romanian owner reports errors of *"17 metri sau mai mult"* ("17 metres or more") and a 2D-only view, no floor display.

## Battery

740 mAh. Claimed: 24-48 h; imoo's storefronts publish no figure, but its own Amazon copy says 1-2 days moderate use (corrected 2026-08-19). Observed: 24-48 h, corrected from 24-72 after the same outlet's second test: *"rund einem Tag"* (about one day). Worst credible: ~24 h intensive, including GPS and video calls.

## Buying

EUR 299 from imoo's EU store, free shipping to Romania, 3-7 business days (accessed 2026-08-19). eMAG: 3,630.00 RON, marketplace seller DeiTim (discount from 3,579.99 lapsed 2026-08-19), roughly 2.3x imoo's price.

## Watch out for

SOS is an opt-in on-screen "C" gesture, no hardware button, inert until enabled in the app. No privacy policy covers the watch service; data goes outside Europe; hosting region UNKNOWN. Camera, chat and "bump and add friends" lack documented off-switches. The app has no Romanian localisation.
