## Verdict

LAMAX does not sell to Romania; the eMAG reseller charges roughly 2.8x the vendor's price. Bands and VoLTE are unpublished even in the CE declaration, the tracking-interval floor is 9 minutes, and the one dated first-hand report placed a child ~2 km from his school.

## Does it work in Romania?

Unknown — no blocking mechanism identified, but zero Romanian user reports and end-to-end use untested. Confirmed 2026-08-19: LAMAX Connect is live in the RO Play and iOS stores; registration is by e-mail, no phone number; any nano-SIM works. No Romanian localisation, support or warranty entity.

## Connectivity

The vendor publishes only "2G, 3G, 4G LTE" — technologies, not bands; even the CE declaration has no frequency table. B20: UNKNOWN. VoLTE: UNKNOWN — never mentioned by LAMAX. Romania has no 3G, so without VoLTE calls and SOS fall back to 2G; data stays on LTE. No owner signal reports exist.

## Positioning

GPS, A-GPS, Wi-Fi and LBS — no GLONASS, Galileo or BeiDou. Vendor figures: GPS 5-30 m, Wi-Fi ~300 m, LBS up to 1000 m. Interval: 9 minutes, 1 hour or manual. One CZ report (2024-11-22, unverified): "Jakmile přišel do školy mi aplikace zobrazila polohu mimo školu, přibližně 2 km."

## Battery

680 mAh. Claimed, four conflicting figures: "up to 120 hours" (at a 60-minute interval), 72 hours (packaging), "2-3 days" (web), "approx. 3-5 days" (manual). Observed: roughly 40 hours, one CZ school-day report (2024-11-22), unverified. Nothing measured at the 9-minute interval.

## Buying

Checked 2026-08-19: 2,249.10 CZK (~€90) at lamax-electronics.com, no shipping to Romania; 1,294.32 RON (~€255) at eMAG.ro, marketplace seller BRANDSLANDIA GROUP (rating 3.9, 0 reviews).

## Watch out for

White-label app (com.ztc.lamax): upstream vendor and data-hosting country UNKNOWN. The App Store privacy label claims no data collection, contradicting the vendor's own privacy policy. A CZ review asserts remote listening; the manual never mentions it — unresolved. Warranty runs against the eMAG reseller (OUG 140/2021), not LAMAX.
