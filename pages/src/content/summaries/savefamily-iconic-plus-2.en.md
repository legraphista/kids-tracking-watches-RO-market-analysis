## Verdict

Ships to Romania — the only proven part. App rated 1.7/5 from 1,832 ratings; backend a Chinese white-label platform exposed at DEF CON 34; voice path undocumented. Do not buy this to solve a Romania problem.

## Does it work in Romania?

Unknown, leaning negative. Proven: shipping ("Rumanía (EUR €)" in the storefront country list) and the app in both Romanian storefronts. All Romanian first-hand reports are connection failures — "Nu te poți conecta cu ceasul copilului" (2024-04-09, 1★) — carried forward, not re-verified; against a 1.7/5 global rating, that is a product problem, not a Romania block. +40 acceptance untested (no country gate documented). No Romanian retailer or warranty entity.

## Connectivity

"Available networks: 4G/3G/H+" — 3G/H+ are dead in Romania. Sole band statement is hedged and brand-wide (support FAQ): "operan generalmente en bandas B3, B7, B20"; B20 — the indoor-coverage band — UNKNOWN for this SKU. "VoLTE" appears in no SaveFamily source; voice fallback (2G CSFB or nothing) undocumented — use Orange or Digi (2G), not Vodafone. No indoor-signal owner report.

## Positioning

GPS + Wi-Fi + LBS (Wi-Fi helps indoors); constellations unknown. Spanish reports (05/06/2023, 27/06/2024): "según el momento la clava o te lo ubica a varios km". No Romanian positioning report.

## Battery

Claimed (850 mAh): "Aprox. 24 horas" moderate use, "8-12 horas" GPS active, "Hasta 36 horas" savings — a regression from gen 1 (1000 mAh, "9-14 horas" GPS active). Observed: UNKNOWN for this model; brand/gen-1 reports ("no le aguanta un día entero") are not Iconic Plus 2 figures.

## Buying

€129 (from €149) at savefamilygps.com; UPS to Romania, free over €76, €10.00 under. Also Amazon.es, TradeInn/Kidinn. No Romanian channel; 72-hour refund claim window. Checked 2026-08-19.

## Watch out for

- DEF CON 34: "39 brands, one server"; data "sits on Alibaba Cloud in China" (no SaveFamily-device exploit shown); privacy policy: no storage location named.
- "Módulo antiacoso" is remote listening; lockability unknown.
- Five dated warranty-denial reports (2022–2024); Play data-safety card: "doesn't collect user data".
