## Verdict

A 295 RON white-label, confirmed only on Digi. No LTE band list, B20 status or VoLTE claim exists; the platform behind it has demonstrated security holes. An experiment inside a return window, not the answer.

## Does it work in Romania?

Verified — tier 3. Alin, 30 Jul 2026: *"merge pe DIGI"* ("it works on DIGI") — the only carrier named in 159 reviews. Orange and Vodafone: unverified; the seller's "all networks" line is marketing. Nano-SIM only; no eSIM.

## Connectivity

No LTE band list, CE/RED document or OEM model number exists. B20 (indoor/rural coverage in Romania): UNKNOWN — biggest unresolved risk; B28: UNKNOWN. VoLTE: never stated; voice fallback UNKNOWN — without it, calls depend on 2G, no committed end date. No owner reports indoor or weak-signal behaviour.

## Positioning

Vendor claims GPS+AGPS+LBS+WiFi (only GPS; no Galileo/GLONASS/BeiDou), unverified. The full review pool (2026-08-19) holds six GPS/position complaints — including *"în celălalt capăt al țării"* ("the other end of the country") — an earlier "no drift complaints" claim was false. Update interval: UNKNOWN.

## Battery

Claimed: 168 h (eMAG spec, conditions unstated). Observed: 2–60 h from 18 dated RO reports; 48–60 h only on a stated minimal profile (monitoring, 2–3 calls/day); only interval-qualified report: 12 h at a 1-hour GPS poll, social apps off; low end: 2–3 h, overheating.

## Buying

295 RON (~€58; claimed PRP 397 RON); in stock on eMAG.ro (2026-08-19); third-party seller "Wirsen", not eMAG 1P. Warranty: 12 months for consumers — below the two-year statutory period.

## Watch out for

- SETracker backend fully open in 2020, clients never fixed; Black Hat (Aug 2026, secondary): hijackable with an email alone, data to a mainland-China cloud.
- *"Monitorizare spion"* (covert listening) is marketed; camera, games, Google Play not disableable (Family Link: one unverified report).
- Register SeTracker 2 (+40 number) before paying; disable the SIM PIN.
- "IP67" sits beside "0 atm"; assume splash-proof.
- Review-farming in the 5★ pool; 3 of 159 texts report dead units.
