## Verdict

The manual, not the marketing, proves a correct Romanian band set. Risks: 750 mAh, and indoor fallback to cell-tower LBS. No Romanian owner review exists.

## Does it work in Romania?

Status: **likely**. Proven: nano-SIM (no eSIM); Canyon Life live in the RO Play storefront, updated 18 August 2026; eMAG.ro stock. Brand-level: Orange Romania retailed the sibling KW-44 with an Orange nano-SIM as recently as September 2025. Unproven: zero Romanian owner reports; canyon.ro 404s on this SKU.

## Connectivity

LTE B1/2/3/5/7/8/20: B20, decisive for indoor and rural coverage in Romania, present; B28 absent. VoLTE vendor-stated (on-watch toggle), unverified on a Romanian SIM; GSM 900/1800 remains, so failure degrades to 2G voice on Orange or Digi, not silence. One verified Slovak owner reports daily morning network disconnects.

## Positioning

TD1050: GPS, GLONASS, A-GPS, LBS — no Galileo. GPS 1–30 m; indoors it switches to LBS at "100 m or more" and silently shows the last registered location. "Safetrack AI" Wi-Fi scanning is marketing-only, absent from the 98-page manual — UNKNOWN; owners corroborate the pessimistic case (BG, LV). Modes 60/10/1 min; 7-day history.

## Battery

Claimed: "Up to 2 days" at a 10-minute GPS interval, games/audio excluded; standby 120 h with SIM, 168 h without; 750 mAh; charge ≤2.5 h. Observed: UNKNOWN — nothing published. Owners: "almost two days" (SE), a school day (SK, unverified); on Precise mode the battery "drains rapidly" (BG, 2★).

## Buying

529.99 RON on eMAG.ro (blue, lilac; 2026-08-19), marketplace seller "Prestigio Plaza", not eMAG; 30-day return applies. Abroad: osiriss.lv, €88.27.

## Watch out for

- Vendor-admitted SOS defect: voicemail on SOS 1 stops dialling 2 and 3.
- Covert "Silent callback" listening and covert camera survive "Do not disturb"; no off switch.
- "EU-based servers" is marketing; app policy §9.1 permits non-EU storage (operator Perenio IoT, dev4perenio@gmail.com).
- Camera/games lack individual off switches; Heureka.cz reviews look seeded.
