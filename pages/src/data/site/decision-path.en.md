**There is no great option here. Anyone telling you otherwise is selling something.**

1. **SIM first:** any cheap prepaid with voice and ~1 GB of data, preferably **Digi or Orange** — if VoLTE fails, calls fall back to 2G instead of silence. Only the Garmin needs more: its own mandatory ~€10/month subscription.
2. **Buy the [imoo Watch Phone Z7](watches/imoo-z7.md)** — €186 from imoostore.com (ships to Romania) or ~1,115 lei on eMAG (seller OVOLT, 14-day return). Its historical blocker — imoo's +40 registration — was tested first-hand on 2026-08-19 and **passed**: account created, straight to watch pairing, no SMS code even asked for. Redo the free test with your own number before paying.
3. **Want the simplest safe path instead? The [TCL MOVETIME MT48](watches/tcl-movetime-mt48.md)** — 898 lei, sold by eMAG itself, 30-day return, 24-month warranty. Almost no reviews anywhere, so you buy the fewest *known* problems — not the same thing as the fewest problems. Keep the invoice.
4. **On a budget: the [imoo Z3](watches/imoo-z3.md)** — 664 lei on Amazon.de. Same battery as the Z7, but no location history and no Galileo/BeiDou.
5. **Whatever you buy, test inside the return window:** an incoming call while the watch is on LTE, and its reported position from inside a building.
