## Verdict

A product class, not one model: one Chinese ODM family (Wonlex/YiQingTeng, SeTracker2 platform) wearing at least eighteen Romanian labels. It demonstrably calls and video-calls on all four Romanian networks, and fails on battery, hardware lifespan and security: in August 2026 researchers took over a platform watch from the parent's email address alone. Buy only as a disposable experiment with a 30-day return.

## Does it work in Romania?

Verified: dated Romanian owner reports on Digi and Vodafone (2024–2026); kidgps.ro and xkids.ro state Orange/Vodafone/Digi/Telekom compatibility in writing (seller claims, not test results).

## Connectivity

All SKUs run the UNISOC SL8521E (Cat-4), platform-capable of B1/B2/B3/B5/B7/B8/B20/B28A; actual per-SKU bands: UNKNOWN. B20, the band that penetrates buildings, is a build-time option per production order (KT26S: "Optional: B20 B12 B28A"; KT26/KT30 lack it); no Romanian listing publishes bands; missing B20 matches "offline during school hours" complaints. VoLTE: UNKNOWN per SKU; fallback: claimed VoLTE + 2G CSFB, unverified on Romanian networks. Remedy: written CE/RED declaration for the exact SKU, or an in-school Orange-SIM test in the return window.

## Positioning

GPS/AGPS/Wi-Fi/LBS; OEM: GPS 5–15 m, LBS 100–1000 m; no Galileo (permanent, silicon-level). Indoors Wi-Fi dominates: "a child in Venezuela" fixes; location only on home Wi-Fi.

## Battery

Claimed: 48 h (Wonlex KT27, OEM), rising to 130–168 h in Romanian retail copy. Observed: ~1 h to 48–60 h, best at "monitoring only + 2–3 calls a day"; most dated RO reports: 5–24 h with polling active.

## Buying

~50–90 EUR (250–450 RON), 2026-08-19: eMAG.ro marketplace, kidgps.ro, xkids.ro; kidgps.ro states 30-day free returns; recorded 3-year warranty: unverified.

## Watch out for

- Aug 2026 (Black Hat/DEF CON): camera/microphone/location takeover demonstrated; 46 applications, 39 brands, one SETracker server; fix unconfirmed.
- Covert ambient listening ("monitorizare spion"): no documented off switch; third-party-triggered in 2020 and 2026.
- 2026 cloud decay: registration failures, "Dispozitivul este oprit" errors, "666test" cross-account notifications (cause unknown).
- Warranty replacements fail identically; generic 5-star review clusters.
