## Verdict

Romania-complete radio with VoLTE, from a vendor with no Romanian carrier or storefront. No location history outside China; SOS is a gesture, not a button. Romania: unknown.

## Does it work in Romania?

Likely — upgraded 2026-08-19: the shared imoo account flow was completed first-hand with a +40 number (account created, straight to the pair-watch screen — no SMS challenge presented; same app and account system as the Z7). The app is live in both Romanian storefronts (verified 2026-08-19); two Romanian users ran paired watches; the one failed-SMS-code report matched an identical failure from Germany, likely an app bug. Still untested for the Z3 itself: pairing, calls and positioning on a Romanian network.

## Connectivity

FDD-LTE B1/B3/B5/B7/B8/B20/B28 is Romania-complete; B20, the band deciding indoor and rural reach, is present. B28 buys nothing here (Romanian 700 MHz is 5G NR n28, not LTE). VoLTE is supported and required for calling; Romanian VoLTE is a SIM-profile flag: activate it in a phone first. Voice fallback UNKNOWN: 2G CSFB is unconfirmed, so prefer Orange or Digi over Vodafone. A French owner runs the Z3 on Free Mobile, an operator absent from imoo's carrier list, with LTE and working two-way voice (2026-07).

## Positioning

GPS/GLONASS/QZSS/Wi-Fi/LBS; no Galileo, no BeiDou. imoo's EU Data Act disclosure (2025-10-29): no location history outside China, on-demand fixes only. Sole accuracy figure: 10-20 metres (German owner, 2026-07-16). Indoor behaviour untested. Geofence alerts appear Wi-Fi-anchored; one Polish owner says they do not fire.

## Battery

Claimed: imoo's spec tables publish no runtime for the 740 mAh cell; its own Amazon DE title says "1-2 Tage Nutzung" (24-48 h), while louder retail claims of 6 days, 8 days and 168 h conflict and are unsupported. Observed: 48 h across four dated owners, including a French owner after 3 weeks (2026-07-22); a full day's wear ends at more or less 50%; charging 10->60% in 30 min.

## Buying

As of 2026-08-19: Amazon.de (IMOO Direct), RON 663.86, free delivery to Romania, 14-day return — under half eMAG's marketplace 1,522.99 RON. imoo EU store: EUR 126.65, 30-day money-back, 2-year warranty.

## Watch out for

SOS is drawing a "C" on screen; the only button is power, and it calls the parent, not 112. Inbound calls and binding/login are the platform's recurring failures; a 2-hour clock drift returns every 2-4 days. The watch UI has no Romanian; the parent app does. Privacy is vendor-asserted: no EU controller, self-contradicting disclosures, no independent audit. Some Z3-titled Amazon listings mixed in Z1/X10 ratings.
