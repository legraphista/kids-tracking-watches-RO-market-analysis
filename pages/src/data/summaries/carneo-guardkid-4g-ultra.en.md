## Verdict

The Romanian channel is real and the radio clears the floor. Indoors, though, it is a cell-tower device, its backend was broken at DEF CON 34, and battery is one school day, not four.

## Does it work in Romania?

Likely, on tier-2 evidence: CARNEO Guard is verified live on the RO Play storefront and App Store, eMAG.ro has stock, and Bucharest has a service centre (2026-08-19). But zero Romanian owner reports exist, and the RO Play page shows no ratings block.

## Connectivity

B20 (800 MHz) is published for this generation — but as one vendor boilerplate string; no Declaration of Conformity exists. B28A buys nothing here (RO 700 MHz is 5G n28). VoLTE not claimed; voice falls back to 2G CSFB — buy an Orange or Digi SIM. Without working data the watch restarts roughly hourly.

## Positioning

GPS+GLONASS; Galileo is absent at chip level and cannot be added; single-band. Indoors GPS fails and Wi-Fi positioning sleeps — Carneo says Wi-Fi is only for firmware updates — leaving LBS, up to several kilometres out. A Slovak review measured 300–600 m indoors; no owner GNSS capture exists.

## Battery

Claimed: 1000 mAh, up to 4 days / 96 h standby (carneo.eu); the manual says 20–24 h at the 10-minute tracking interval. Observed: 24–48 h across eight reports; worst: "vydrzi sotva jeden den" (barely a day — Alza.cz, 2025-12-22).

## Buying

eMAG.ro, marketplace seller "HomeMode": 865.41 RON, approx. EUR 170 (2026-08-19) — about 34% above the EUR 129 RRP; Slovak street EUR 104.88 (smartshop.sk). Marketplace seller means a 14-day return window.

## Watch out for

- SETracker backend broken at DEF CON 34: read/spoof location, silent listening; no Carneo response.
- The Guard privacy policy names AutoNavi (Alibaba) and ByteDance/Tencent/Baidu SDKs; Play's Data Safety card claims no sharing.
- A documented covert listen mode makes the watch look switched off.
- Android 8.1 (2017); app rated 2.7/5 (Play SK); the child can install apps unseen; SMS cannot be disabled.
- Reviews seeded via a paid 10-unit campaign; the unseeded Alza pool: 3.9/5, 3 of 30 hardware failures, adult-sized body.
