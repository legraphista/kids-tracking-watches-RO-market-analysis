## Verdict

Spec fits Romania; charging is nightly. The historical risk — the account flow — fell on 2026-08-19: a +40 registration completed first-hand, straight to the pair-watch screen (no SMS challenge presented). What remains untested is the watch itself on a Romanian network: run the week-one call and location checks inside the return window.

## Does it work in Romania?

Status: likely — upgraded 2026-08-19, when this research ran the registration test first-hand: an account was created on a +40 number and the app went straight to the pair-watch screen (no SMS challenge was presented; an earlier version of this summary said the SMS code arrived — corrected the same day). That supersedes the one earlier dated failure (RO App Store, 13 Jul 2025). Also in its favour: bands fit, imoo's EU store ships to Romania, eMAG lists it (via OVOLT România), app live in both RO storefronts (not in Romanian). Still untested: pairing, calls and positioning on a Romanian network. Two independent Romanian reports show the same time-zone desync.

## Connectivity

LTE B1/B3/B8/B20 plus B34/B38/B39/B40/B41 (TDD). B20, the indoor/rural band for Romania, is present. VoLTE is stated by imoo but unproven on a Romanian SIM; voice falls back to 2G, so prefer Orange or Digi (both keep 2G). One Romanian review (20 May 2025) reports very poor signal and failed calls.

## Positioning

GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo, QZSS, plus Wi-Fi, LBS, barometer. Background reporting is hourly; on-demand fixes are immediate; Historical Trajectory (off by default, Z7-only outside China) samples GPS every 2 seconds. Tracking requires an active SIM. Indoor accuracy: untested.

## Battery

Claimed: "1 to 2 days" (740 mAh), only on imoo's own Amazon listings, reused across models (corrected entry). Observed: 12% left after 24 hours (Notebookcheck, 04.04.2025, a light day). Owner "2 days" figures include the factory overnight power-off; retailer "7 days"/"240 hours" claims are unsupported.

## Buying

As of 2026-08-19: imoo EU store €186.15 (from €219.00), free shipping to Romania; eMAG.ro 1,114.56 RON (~€218) via marketplace seller OVOLT România — 14-day return, 24-month warranty, zero reviews.

## Watch out for

- SOS is a gesture, not a button; a stressed child may not manage it (unverified).
- Auto Answer (off by default) connects a parent's call after 10 seconds: an open microphone.
- Data jurisdiction unknown; schema includes school name, home address, home Wi-Fi password.
- No verified permanent camera or friend-adding off; seeded reviews and removal solicitations documented.
