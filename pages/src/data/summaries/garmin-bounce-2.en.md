## Verdict

Romania is on Garmin's Bounce 2 coverage table, Romanian buyers have paid Garmin for the LTE service, and the band set fits every Romanian operator — but four detailed 2026 Romanian reviews report calls mostly failing (9 of 40; 1–2 of 10), late messages, and late-or-absent location. Buy only inside eMAG's 30-day return window.

## Does it work in Romania?

Verified (Tier 1, re-checked 2026-08-19): the en-US and ro-RO Garmin coverage pages both list România, with the disclaimer *"does not provide a guarantee of coverage"*. Romanian owners activated and paid the subscription — no geo-block. Which Romanian network the embedded eSIM roams onto: UNKNOWN.

## Connectivity

LTE bands: B3, B8, B20 (owner's manual). B20 — the band that decides indoor and cell-edge coverage in Romania — is present, so the radio is not why it fails. VoLTE: UNKNOWN; the EU DoC cites no 2G/3G standard — unverified inference: no fallback, so voice must be VoLTE or VoWiFi. One Romanian owner: fine in bigger cities; in weak signal it *"se transformă... într-o brățară ce măsoară pași"*.

## Positioning

No constellation list is published; GLONASS/Galileo/BeiDou: UNKNOWN. Fix time: *"30 to 60 seconds"* (manual). The documented failure is latency — location often does not update — and no update interval is published. Romanian reviewer, 2026-03-24: *"Locatia ti-o arata cu mari intarzieri sau deloc"*.

## Battery

Claimed: *"up to 2 days of battery life between charges"* (48 h). Observed: 24-36 h — TechRadar (UK, 2026-04, two months): *"just over a day"*; Front Pack Sports (US, LTE and location pings on): *"a day and a half, tops"*. One owner saw 48-60 h until firmware 16.38, then ~35% overnight drain.

## Buying

eMAG Romania, first-party, 24-month warranty: 1,835.87 RON new / 1,459.99 lei refurbished (2026-08-19); also Altex.ro and Vexio.ro (1,499 lei). Mandatory Garmin subscription, ~EUR 10/month; the Romanian price is unverified (9.99 vs 10.99 EUR quoted).

## Watch out for

- Identical complaints from UK, Polish, Irish and US owners — likely a platform problem no operator choice fixes; eMAG rating 2.50/5 from 6 reviews.
- eSIM only, no SIM slot — no fallback if Garmin withdraws the service; declared security support until 17 September, 2027.
- SOS *"does not contact emergency services on your behalf"*; Kid Mode default passcode 0000.
