## Verdict

Romania-complete published bands (B20 included) from Canyon's own spec page — but eSIM-only, settled with high confidence. Canyon sells no SIM or plan, and no Romanian operator is known to issue a profile this watch can install. Blocked, not condemned: treat as unbuyable until demonstrated.

## Does it work in Romania?

Unknown; evidence tier 5 (bands fit, primary-sourced — necessary, not sufficient). eSIM-only is settled by six primary sources, decisively Canyon's spec field `SIM Card Type: eSIM` versus `Nano-SIM` on the KW-48. Orange: Number Share only (2.03 €/month; Apple/Samsung/Huawei watches only). Digi: prepaid eSIM with its own number, no device list. Vodafone: unchecked. Whether Canyon Life accepts a Romanian activation code is entirely unverified. No first-hand reports exist, in any language.

## Connectivity

FDD-LTE B1/B2/B3/B5/B7/B8/B20/B28A; TDD B38/B39/B40/B41. B20, the band that decides indoor coverage, is present. B28A buys nothing here (700 MHz is 5G NR n28, not LTE); WCDMA is dead weight (Romania has no 3G). VoLTE is vendor-stated, not confirmed provisioned on any Romanian network; if it fails, quad-band GSM degrades voice to 2G on Orange or Digi rather than going silent. No owner-reported signal behaviour exists.

## Positioning

GPS + Wi-Fi + LBS only; no GLONASS, Galileo or BeiDou published. "CANYON AI SafeTrack" is a Wi-Fi scan with no accuracy figure. No observed data. KW-44 owners (same app, same backend) reported GPS stuck at the home address and 50 km errors — cloud-layer failures that transfer.

## Battery

Claimed: 900 mAh; "up to 3 days" use, "up to 5 days" standby; ~71 h on a fully published vendor test profile (10-minute geolocation, daily 5-minute video call). Observed: UNKNOWN — no independent figure. eMAG's spec table ("72 h", "1.3 inch") is wrong.

## Buying

As of 2026-08-19: eMAG.ro marketplace seller "OVOLT România" (not 1P; 14-day return), 650.53 RON, reduced from 797.22 RON (~€131). Cheaper abroad: €67.30 (Heureka.sk), €68.50 (Axdata.sk).

## Watch out for

- "Stealth calls" (covert listen-in) and remote photos: marketed, no documented lock.
- "EU-based servers" is contradicted by the privacy policy ("outside the European Union"); data region UNKNOWN.
- App by Perenio IoT, not Canyon; iOS build last updated 2024-08-04; Contact Info "used to track you".
- No CE/RED declaration or manual found; Unisoc CVE fit to the UMS9117-L unverified; KW-44 "device is not allowed" pairing errors, same cloud.
