## Verdict

Both gates resolved in its favour: bands Romania-complete, TCL Connect live in the Romanian Play storefront. Untested: +40 registration and VoLTE provisioning. New concern: five owner-reported hardware deaths inside four months.

## Does it work in Romania?

Status: likely. TCL Connect (`com.trackerandroid.trackerandroid`) is in the Romanian Play store, updated 2026-07-17, localised in Romanian; its two Romanian reviews are pairing failures on older models. eMAG sells it first-party, 24-month warranty, 30-day returns. +40 registration is unobserved and no Romanian owner found — test registration free before buying.

## Connectivity

TCL's RED declaration confirms LTE B1/3/7/8/20, including B20, the low band that decides indoor and rural coverage, plus GSM 900/1800 — so on Orange or Digi voice falls back to 2G if VoLTE fails. VoLTE is vendor-stated, unverified on Romanian networks. One owner never got voice and video both working across three operators; APN may need manual setup.

## Positioning

Dual-band GPS L1+L5 (vendor claim; Airoha GNSS module per TCL's manual); other constellations unknown; no independent accuracy test. Owners praise it outdoors; cellular fallback runs around 200 m, indoor geofences flap on WiFi/LBS, and two repeat-TCL buyers saw no gain over prior models.

## Battery

Claimed: 900 mAh, 60 h use / 168 h standby (TCL internal lab tests). Observed: ">24 h" — "räcker mer än en dag" (M3.se, updated 2026-05-02, no conditions); seven later owner reports say roughly one day; one measured 36–40 h. Charge nightly.

## Buying

eMAG.ro, first-party ("Vândut și livrat de eMAG"), 897.99 RON, "În stoc", checked 2026-08-19; RRP €150 (press release, 2025-09-10). Altex and Flanco list nothing.

## Watch out for

- Five hardware deaths inside four months, in nine dated reports.
- Children's data: Shenzhen, China controller; China/US service providers; ISO 27701/27001 claims unverified.
- 2020 account-takeover on the older MOVETIME Family Watch.
- App rating 3.8★/16,600 blends TCL routers.
- Contacts capped at 20; one owner contradicts IP68.
